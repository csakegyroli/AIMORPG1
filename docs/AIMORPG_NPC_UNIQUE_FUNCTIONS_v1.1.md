# AIMORPG — NPC Unique Functions v1.1

## Design rule
Every region offers at least one inn and one blacksmith. NPC functions are local gameplay surfaces, not generic buttons. The player acts through item cards, detail panels, selection and contextual actions.

## Universal basics
- Every region: lodging + food + a smithing/crafting station.
- Inns: choose 1/2/4/8 hours; 1 hour restores 10% max HP; cost is paid in gold and time.
- Food: selected visually and consumed for percentage-based HP recovery.
- Forge: craft from regional materials, select targets visually, dismantle owned equipment into materials.
- Vendors: regional stock with item icons, rarity, category, price and detail view.
- No raw “Use function” language; the action is contextual to the NPC’s role.
