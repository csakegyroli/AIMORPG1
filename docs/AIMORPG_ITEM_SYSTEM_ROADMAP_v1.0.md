# AIMORPG — Item System Roadmap v1.0

## Canonical direction
Items are a major pillar of the game, not filler loot. Every region gets a distinct acquisition ecosystem and thematic item pool.

### Regional market model
- Each major region gets its own market / acquisition layer.
- Regional sellers are thematic and location-bound.
- Some items are exclusive to specific regions, NPCs, quests, professions or social routes.
- Prices and access can depend on reputation, relationships, region access and world state.
- The global player market remains a separate system from local NPC commerce.

### Item categories to expand
- everyday consumables and food/drink
- weapons and armor
- tools and profession equipment
- trade goods and materials
- quest items
- evidence / documents / keys
- prestige and social items
- gambling / entertainment tokens
- travel and maritime equipment
- crafting components
- rare lore / mystery items

### Quest items
Quest items should be first-class content with explicit provenance, purpose, and world reaction. Some may be tradeable, stolen, forged, destroyed, or revealed through investigation depending on quest rules.

### NPC unique functions
Every major NPC should eventually have one meaningful local function (shop, service, training, minigame, information, crafting, transport, gambling, lodging, etc.). Functions are available on the map, not inside the global directory.

### Design constraints
- preserve the existing perfect tooltip / item-card systems;
- expand content before adding complexity;
- avoid a universal shop where every region sells everything;
- regional item pools should reinforce the fantasy and story of each region.
