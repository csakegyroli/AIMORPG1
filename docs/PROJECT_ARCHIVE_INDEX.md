# AIMORPG — Project Archive Index

This clean release contains the current source plus the durable design specifications from the entire project. Historical release ZIPs and duplicate release notes are intentionally excluded from the runnable package.

## Major milestones

- v0.16–v1.7: interface stabilization, item/tooltips, NPC local interaction, server clock, regional map architecture.
- v1.8–v1.18: six capital regions, regional gates, main-quest following, chronicle prologue, NPC presence and time rules.
- v1.19: Quest DNA, Canon/Dynamic layers, dormant Narrative Director foundation.
- v1.20–v1.27: expanded item system, regional sets, NPC unique functions, crafting, dismantling, alchemy, reward economy foundations.
- v1.28–v1.33: 60-part Black Coin main story deepening, quest objectives, navigation freedom, NPC backstory discovery.
- v1.34–v1.37: deterministic side-quest/dialogue/world-event runtime, AI Council foundation, player-facing narrative runtime.
- v1.38–v1.39: Reward/Loot/Economy Engine, hidden pity/fortune protection, 365-day Kingdom Calendar and Canon Event Calendar.

## Non-negotiable design principles

1. The map is the primary play surface.
2. The player sees consequences, not hidden mathematics.
3. NPC presence is determined by place and world time.
4. Canon is authored; Dynamic content may not silently contradict Canon.
5. AI remains dormant until the whole world is ready.
6. Rare items are economically and statistically rare, not merely numerically strong.
