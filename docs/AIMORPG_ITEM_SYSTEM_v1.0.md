# AIMORPG Item System v1.0

## Purpose
The item system is now a world-facing economic layer, not just a collection of equipment pieces. Items carry category, origin, legal status, trade value, market eligibility and quest-binding information.

## Regional economies
- Külváros: survival, workshops, street trade, documents and early materials
- Aréna: fighting supplies, betting objects, champion prestige items
- Kereskedelmi Központ: trade goods, logistics, guild documents, ledgers
- Belváros: luxury, gaming, debt and status goods
- Palota: restricted official documents, seals, diplomatic and archival objects
- Kikötő: food, sail materials, navigation, contraband and cargo evidence

## Player-visible rule
The player sees consequences and meaningful labels, not hidden economic formulas. Item details expose origin, category and legal status.

## Quest items
Quest items are never sold through normal market flows. Their existence can still be visible in inventory because the player may need to carry evidence, keys, documents or story objects.

## Regional market rule
The global player market and the current region's NPC/city stock are separate layers. The city stock follows the player's current region and only exposes items belonging to that region's pool.

## Future expansion
This system is intentionally prepared for NPC unique functions, item-linked quest choices, crafting, minigames, contraband, evidence chains and later AI side-quest generation.
