# AIMORPG — SIDE QUEST FRAMEWORK v1.0

## Status

Designed and structurally implemented. Generation is disabled until the final AI release gate.

## Sources of side-quest pressure

1. **Relationship** — an NPC's trust, suspicion, debt, fear or affection becomes narratively actionable.
2. **Local problem** — something in the current district needs attention.
3. **Night opportunity** — a night-shift NPC can keep the world moving when daytime contacts are unavailable.
4. **World reaction** — an earlier player action creates a believable response.
5. **Identity reflection** — the player's repeated behaviour creates a natural type of problem.

## Admission requirements

A side quest must identify:

- NPC owner;
- current place;
- current reason;
- player-specific relevance;
- one meaningful choice;
- at least one durable consequence;
- canon references;
- fail-forward behaviour where appropriate.

## Anti-spam rules

- no side quest simply because the player has nothing to do;
- no duplicate quest premise without a changed reason;
- no automatic generation after every interaction;
- no daily pressure that punishes absence;
- night-time content keeps the world alive but does not become mandatory grind.

## Future AI contract

The future AI may turn a Director hook into a side-quest proposal. A deterministic validator must reject anything that violates canon, NPC motivation, region access, time availability, player state or consequence rules.
