# AIMORPG — MAIN QUEST AUDIT v1.0

## Purpose

The 60-node main story is now treated as a set of authored regional arcs plus a campaign gate system. The player is not locked into a single global order, but each regional story remains sequential. Regional access is earned through a combination of main-story progress, reputation, wealth, relationship and identity signals.

## Chapter mastery

A regional main arc is **mastered at 95% completion**. Mastery is a narrative milestone, not a requirement to start every next region. Because each arc has its own internal chain, the player still needs to progress meaningfully rather than unlock a region after one or two early nodes.

| Chapter | Region | Quest count | Mastery | Primary role |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| 1 | Külváros | 24 | 95% | teaches the city's social grammar and reveals the first Black Coin evidence |
| 2 | Aréna | 8 | 95% | converts personal capability into public reputation and exposes betting manipulation |
| 3 | Kereskedelmi Központ | 10 | 95% | maps money, logistics, debt and shipment flows |
| 4 | Belváros | 7 | 95% | turns money into social access, debt and political influence |
| 5 | Királyi Palota | 5 | 95% | makes the Black Coin an official state investigation |
| 6 | Kikötő | 6 | 95% | converts the city mystery into a transregional maritime network |

## Regional access model

### Külváros
Always available. This is the shared starting point.

### Aréna
Requires:
- at least 75% of the Külváros main arc;
- at least 8 reputation;
- at least 30 gold;
- a combat-oriented capability signal or equivalent Külváros progress.

Narrative meaning: the player has demonstrated enough capability that the Arena is willing to take them seriously.

### Kereskedelmi Központ
Requires:
- at least 50% of the Külváros main arc;
- at least 10 reputation;
- at least 120 gold;
- a trade-oriented capability signal or sufficient Külváros economic progress.

Narrative meaning: the player has demonstrated that they can survive the city's economic layer and can afford entry.

### Belváros
Requires:
- the complete Arena arc **or** the complete Commercial Center arc;
- at least 18 reputation;
- at least 750 gold;
- a strong relationship with an Arena contact.

Narrative meaning: status is not purchased by gold alone. The player needs a credible social bridge.

### Királyi Palota
Requires:
- the complete Belváros arc;
- at least 35 reputation;
- at least 2,500 gold;
- a strong relationship with a Belvárosi contact.

Narrative meaning: political access is earned through relevance and relationships, not only wealth.

### Kikötő
The Harbor deliberately offers multiple routes:
- at least 80% of the Commercial Center arc;
- **or** at least 50% of the Belváros arc;
- **or** completion of the Palace arc;
- plus at least 15 reputation and 100 gold.

Narrative meaning: the Harbor is the destination for commerce gone too far, wealth lost, political stories escaping into the world, and honest or criminal reinvention.

## What 95% completion means

95% is treated as **story mastery**, not a hidden numeric score shown to the player. The player sees natural language and chapter states such as:

- Megnyílt
- Folyamatban
- Közel a lezáráshoz
- Lezárva
- A fejezet teljes képe feltárult

The mathematics remains a background system.

## Why the gate system is not arbitrary

Each gate answers a world question:

- Arena: have you proven yourself?
- Market: can you function economically?
- Inner City: have you built enough status and connection?
- Palace: do you matter politically?
- Harbor: where does your story go when the center is no longer your home?

## Main quest audit principles

1. Every quest belongs to a region and an NPC.
2. Every quest should reveal a distinct part of the Black Coin mystery.
3. Every quest provides a method signal for player identity.
4. Every completed quest can leave a world memory.
5. Every regional arc has a meaningful completion state.
6. No region is opened merely because the player completed one or two quests in another region.
7. Failure does not dead-end the main story; the method and consequences are retained.
8. The next region can be reached through more than one legitimate life path when appropriate.

## Remaining audit pass

Before AI side quests are designed, each of the 60 quests must be reviewed once more for:
- NPC motivation coherence;
- location-specific gameplay;
- consequence strength;
- relationship effects;
- identity signals;
- world-memory tags;
- regional gate relevance;
- future item/quest-item hooks.
